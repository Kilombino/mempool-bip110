Your Security Hashrate YSH

Your YSH is the break-even point between what you mine and what you hold in Bitcoin-Blake2b: the network's hashrate shared out among every coin in circulation, multiplied by the coins you keep. It is the share of the chain's security that your own money calls for.

The idea is to stay close to it. Falling well short means your money is being guarded by people other than you; overshooting by a lot means you are paying to guard other people's money.

The network hashrate is an estimate from recent blocks, so it wobbles a little between readings; that is why the balance verdict is given with a wide margin, give or take 25 %. The supply is what has been issued up to the current height, adding up the subsidy of each halving epoch.
